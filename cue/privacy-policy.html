Privacy Policy

Cue (Android package com.rivontech.cue; iOS bundle com.rivontech.cue) · Effective 30 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Cue (the "App"), a reminder app for bills, subscriptions and other recurring payments, provided by RivonTech ("RivonTech", "we", "us"), an independent developer based in the United Kingdom. RivonTech is the controller of the personal information described here.

1. Summary

Cue works fully on your device without an account. Your reminders, pockets and settings are stored on your phone, and notifications are scheduled by your phone. If you choose to sign in, we receive your name and email address from Google or Apple. If you subscribe to Cue Plus and turn on cloud backup, a copy of your reminders, pockets and settings is stored in our cloud database so you can restore it. Cue has no advertising and no analytics, and we do not sell your information.

2. Information stored only on your device

The App stores the following on your device: reminders (name, optional amount, schedule, reminder times, paid and muted status), pockets (name, colour, icon and optional balance), and settings (theme, accent colour, currency, reminder time, notification sound and the name you enter on your profile). This data is used to show your reminders and schedule notifications. Unless you use cloud backup, it is not sent to RivonTech.

Notifications are scheduled on your device by its operating system. If you choose a notification sound from your device's storage, the App copies that file into your device's notification sounds so the system can play it; the file is not uploaded.

You can delete everything using Settings → Erase all reminders & pockets, or by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling it.

3. Signing in (optional)

You can sign in with Google or, on iPhone, with Apple. Signing in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication. We receive:

InformationPurpose
Your name and email address from Google or AppleIdentifying your account and showing your name in the App
An account identifier created by Firebase, and your Google or Apple account identifierLinking your cloud backup and subscription to you
Sign-in times and technical details of the sign-in request, such as IP addressSecurity, abuse prevention and keeping your account working

If you use Sign in with Apple and choose to hide your email, we receive an Apple relay address instead of your real one. We never receive your Google or Apple password.

4. Cue Plus and cloud backup

Cue Plus is an optional subscription. While you are signed in with Cue Plus and cloud backup is switched on, the App stores a copy of your reminders, pockets and settings (as listed in section 2) in Google Cloud Firestore. It is linked to your account, kept in data centres in the European Union, and protected so that only your signed-in account can read or change it. The copy is used to restore your data after you reinstall the App or move to a new device. You can turn cloud backup off at any time in your profile; turning it off stops new uploads.

5. Purchases

Subscriptions are bought through Google Play or the Apple App Store, which handle payment. We do not receive your card details. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives your account identifier and purchase information from the store, such as the product, purchase and renewal dates, and the store's transaction identifiers. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

6. Security checks

To protect accounts and backups from misuse, the App uses Firebase App Check, which asks Google Play Integrity (Android) or Apple App Attest and DeviceCheck (iOS) to confirm that requests come from a genuine copy of the App on a real device. These services process device and app integrity signals under their own policies.

7. Service providers

We use these providers to run the App's optional online features. They process information on our behalf or under their own terms:

  • Google (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, App Check, Google Sign-In): Google Privacy Policy and Firebase privacy information
  • Apple (Sign in with Apple, App Store): Apple Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat (subscription status): RevenueCat Privacy Policy

We do not use advertising networks or analytics services, and we do not sell or rent your personal information.

8. Permissions

The App may ask to send notifications so it can remind you before something is due, and uses internet access for sign-in, cloud backup and purchases. It may use vibration for feedback and, when you choose a sound file, access to the file you pick. The App does not request your location, contacts, camera or microphone.

9. Legal bases

Where the UK GDPR, EU GDPR or similar laws apply, we rely on: performance of our contract with you to provide your account, cloud backup and Cue Plus; our legitimate interests in keeping the App secure and preventing abuse; and legal obligations, for example keeping records of purchases where required.

10. Retention and deletion

Data on your device stays until you erase it, clear the App's storage or uninstall. Your account and cloud backup are kept until you delete them. You can delete your account in the App under Profile → Delete account: this permanently removes your account and your cloud backup, while reminders on your phone remain. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription; cancel it in your Google Play or App Store account settings. Store and RevenueCat purchase records are kept according to their own policies and legal requirements.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to complain to a regulator. Most of this you can do directly in the App. For anything else, email us at the address below and we will respond within 30 days. UK residents may complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

12. International transfers

Your cloud backup is stored in the European Union. Authentication and some processing by Google, Apple and RevenueCat may take place in other countries, including the United States, under the safeguards described in their policies, such as standard contractual clauses.

13. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

14. Security

Information sent between the App and our providers is encrypted in transit, cloud backups are only accessible to the signed-in account that owns them, and access is checked on the server. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and will act promptly on any issue we become aware of.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this Policy when the App, its providers or the law change. The current version and its effective date will always be on this page. If a change is significant, we will also tell you in the App or on the store listing.

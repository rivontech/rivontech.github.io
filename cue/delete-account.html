Delete your Cue account

Cue by RivonTech · Android and iOS

In the app

  1. Open Cue and tap your profile picture (top right of Home), or go to Settings and tap your name.
  2. Scroll to Account and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm with Google (or Apple on iPhone).

This permanently deletes your account and your Cue Plus cloud backup straight away. Reminders stored on your phone are not affected; remove them with Settings → Erase all reminders & pockets or by uninstalling Cue.

Without the app

Email vnomapps@gmail.com from the email address you sign in with, with the subject "Delete my Cue account". We will delete your account and cloud backup within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: your account (name, email, sign-in identifiers) and your cloud backup of reminders, pockets and settings.
  • Kept: purchase records held by Google Play, the App Store and RevenueCat, which they keep under their own policies and legal requirements.

Deleting your account does not cancel a Cue Plus subscription. Cancel it in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions) or in your Apple ID settings.

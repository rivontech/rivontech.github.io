Privacy Policy

vnomedits Facebook Page (Page ID 1345294062010102) · Effective 21 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you interact with vnomedits (the "Page"), a digital creator Page publishing short animated educational videos about our planet, countries, maps, history, wars and world affairs, science and how things are built. The Page is run by RivonTech ("we", "us"), an independent creator based in the United Kingdom, which is the controller of the personal information described here.

1. Summary

We only see what Facebook shows to every Page owner. We use it to run the Page and reply to you. We do not sell your information, we do not keep our own database of followers and we do not use tracking tools of our own.

3. How we use information

  • To publish videos and keep the Page running
  • To reply to comments and messages, including topic requests and the questions our videos invite you to answer
  • To fix mistakes you point out in our videos
  • To keep the Page safe: hiding spam, hate or abusive content and blocking users who break our Terms or Meta's Community Standards
  • To measure what works so we can make better videos

We may show a comment or your public name inside a video or post (for example, answering a viewer's question). If you do not want this, tell us and we will not feature it, or we will remove it.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade your information, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. Information may be shared only where the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of the Page, its viewers or others.

5. Meta and other platforms

The Page runs on Facebook, operated by Meta Platforms, which collects and processes your data under its own Privacy Policy. Your Facebook settings control what Meta shows to Pages. vnomedits videos also appear on Instagram, TikTok and YouTube; your activity there is governed by those platforms' own privacy policies.

6. Monetisation and ads

The Page may earn money through Meta's creator monetisation programmes, such as ads shown on our videos and reels, and may in future feature sponsored content or brand partnerships. Ads on our videos are chosen and shown by Meta, not by us. Meta decides which ads you see based on its own data and your ad settings, under the Meta Privacy Policy; you can manage this in your Facebook Ad Preferences. We do not receive personal information about who sees or clicks those ads, only total earnings and anonymous performance figures. Any sponsored content will be clearly labelled as a paid partnership.

7. Legal bases

Where the UK GDPR, EU GDPR or similar laws apply, we rely on our legitimate interests in running, moderating and improving the Page and replying to you; your consent where you choose to message us or ask to be featured; and legal obligations where they apply.

8. Retention

Comments and messages stay on Facebook until you or we delete them. We do not copy them into our own systems. Any notes we make to handle a request are deleted once the request is complete.

9. Your rights and deletion

You can delete your own comments and messages on Facebook at any time, and unfollow the Page whenever you like. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict processing, and to complain to a regulator. To ask us to delete anything relating to you, or for any other request, email us at the address below and we will respond within 30 days. UK residents may complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

10. Children

Our videos are made for a general audience, but Facebook is not for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Security

Access to manage the Page is limited to its owner. We will act promptly on any security issue we become aware of.

12. Changes

We may update this Policy. The current version and its effective date will always be on this page.

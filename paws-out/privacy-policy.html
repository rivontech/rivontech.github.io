Privacy Policy

Paws Out (Android, package com.rivontech.pawsout) · Effective 28 October 2026

This policy explains what information is used when you play Paws Out, a mobile game made by RivonTech ("we", "us"), an independent developer based in the United Kingdom. It applies to players in every country where the game is available. We built the game so that we don't collect personal information ourselves. The game shows ads, and Google processes some information to do that, as described below.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. Paws Out has no accounts or sign-in, and doesn't ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or location. We don't run our own servers or analytics that receive data from the game. There are no in-app purchases.

2. Information stored on your device

Your game progress stays on your phone and is never sent to us. That includes the Maze Escape days and Cat Jump levels you've unlocked and completed, and your settings (music, sound effects, vibration).

You can erase it any time by uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android settings.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Paws Out is free and is supported by ads served by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK:

  • A banner ad at the bottom of the screen, placed so it never covers the game.
  • Interstitial (full-screen) ads occasionally between levels.

To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process the following from your device:

InformationUsed for
Advertising ID (Android's resettable ad identifier)Showing ads, limiting how often you see the same ad, measuring results and, where allowed, personalising ads

Google processes this information under its own privacy policy. Learn more at policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. It's sent over encrypted connections.

4. Your choices about ads

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: the first time you open the game, Google's consent form asks whether you agree to personalised ads and the related data use. You can change your choice any time with Ad Privacy Choices at the bottom of the game's Settings. If you don't consent, you may still see ads, but they won't be personalised.
  • US states with privacy laws (for example California): you can opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for targeted advertising using your phone's ad settings below. We don't sell personal information ourselves.
  • Everyone: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings: Settings → Privacy (or Google) → Ads.

5. Children

Paws Out is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you're a parent or guardian and think your child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us and we'll help remove it.

6. Permissions

The game uses internet and network-state access to load ads, the advertising ID permission for ads as described above, and vibration for feedback (you can turn it off in Settings). It doesn't access your camera, microphone, contacts, files or location.

7. Your rights, wherever you live

Depending on where you live, data protection laws give you rights over your personal information. These include the GDPR (EU/EEA), UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018 (UK), Swiss data protection law, US state privacy laws (for example California's CCPA/CPRA), Brazil's LGPD, Canada's PIPEDA, and similar laws in other countries.

  • Access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and portability: because we hold no personal data, there's nothing of yours on our side to give or delete. Your game progress is only on your device (section 2).
  • Data held by Google: to access or delete advertising data Google holds, use your Google Account and Google's privacy tools at myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy.
  • Complaints: you can complain to your local data protection authority. In the UK that's the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). We'd appreciate the chance to help first, so please contact us.

International transfers: Google may process advertising data in countries other than yours, including the United States, protected by safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.

8. Keeping and deleting data

We don't hold any of your data. Progress on your device stays until you clear the app's data or uninstall the game. For data processed by Google for advertising, see Google's policies linked above, or reset your advertising ID as described in section 4.

9. Changes to this policy

If the game changes how it handles information, we'll update this page and its effective date.

Privacy Policy

Chaos Drift (Android, package com.rivontech.chaosdrift) · Effective 19 October 2026

This policy explains what information is used when you play Chaos Drift, a mobile game made by RivonTech ("we", "us"), an independent developer based in the United Kingdom. It applies to players in every country where the game is available. We built the game so that we don't collect personal information ourselves. The game does show ads, and the advertising partner described below processes some information to do that.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. Chaos Drift has no accounts or sign-in, and doesn't ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location. We don't run our own servers or analytics that receive data from the game.

2. Information stored on your device

Your game progress stays on your phone and is never sent to us. That includes coins, vehicles you own, the vehicle you've selected, stars, best times, map choice and settings (sound, graphics, controls).

You can erase it any time with Settings → Game → Reset in the game, or by uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android settings.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Chaos Drift is free and is supported by ads served by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK:

  • Banner ads on the menus and at the top of the screen during races.
  • Interstitial (full-screen) ads occasionally between levels.
  • Rewarded ads, only when you choose to watch one: free coins, an extra life, or a doubled race reward.

To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process the following from your device:

Google processes this information under its own privacy policy. Learn more at policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. It's sent over encrypted connections.

4. Your choices about ads

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: the first time you open the game, Google's consent form asks whether you agree to personalised ads and the related data use. You can change your choice any time in Settings → Game → Privacy. If you don't consent, you may still see ads, but they won't be personalised.
  • US states with privacy laws (for example California): where required, a privacy message lets you opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for targeted advertising.
  • Everyone: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings: Settings → Privacy (or Google) → Ads.

5. Children

Chaos Drift is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you're a parent or guardian and think your child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us and we'll help remove it.

6. Permissions

The game uses internet and network-state access to load ads, the advertising ID permission for ads as described above, and vibration for crash feedback (you can turn it off in Settings). It doesn't access your camera, microphone, contacts, files or precise location.

7. Your rights, wherever you live

Chaos Drift is available worldwide. Depending on where you live, data protection laws give you rights over your personal information. These include the GDPR (EU/EEA), UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018 (UK), Swiss data protection law, US state privacy laws (for example California's CCPA/CPRA), Brazil's LGPD, Canada's PIPEDA, and similar laws in other countries.

  • Access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and portability: because we hold no personal data, there's nothing of yours on our side to give or delete. Your game progress is only on your device (section 2).
  • Consent and withdrawing it: for personalised ads in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, the legal basis is your consent, which you can withdraw any time in the game's privacy settings (section 4). Non-personalised ads rely on the legitimate interest of keeping the game free and preventing ad fraud.
  • Opting out of "sale" or "sharing" (US): where state law applies, use the privacy message in the game or your phone's ad settings. We don't sell personal information ourselves.
  • Data held by Google: to access or delete advertising data Google holds, use your Google Account and Google's privacy tools at myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy.
  • Complaints: you can complain to your local data protection authority. In the UK that's the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). We'd appreciate the chance to help first, so please contact us.

International transfers: Google may process advertising data in countries other than yours, including the United States. Google protects these transfers with safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.

8. Keeping and deleting data

We don't hold any of your data. Progress on your device stays until you reset it or uninstall the game. For data processed by Google for advertising, see Google's policies linked above, or reset your advertising ID as described in section 4.

9. Changes to this policy

If the game changes how it handles information, we'll update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the game's store listing.
